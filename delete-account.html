My Sirat - Delete Your Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your My Sirat account and its data. My Sirat is developed by Ashick Labs.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to ashicklabs@gmail.com with the subject "My Sirat Account Deletion".
  2. Write the email address you used to sign up for My Sirat.
  3. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Data that is deleted

  • Your account (email address and login details)
  • Your daily faith progress and app activity data
  • Any other data linked to your account

Data that is kept

We do not keep any personal data after deletion, except anonymous crash or diagnostic data that cannot identify you, if applicable.
